PALMED OFF

Fans furious as Cole Palmer avoids red card for slamming into Mateus Fernandes... but then sent off minutes later

Daisy Redhead, Sports Reporter

Published: 19:15, 24 Oct 2026 | Updated: 19:52, 24 Oct 2026

Add us as a preferred source

COLE PALMER was sent for an early bath just minutes after escaping a red card for slamming Mateus Fernandes.

A rainy day in West London proved disastrous for 21-year-old Mike Penders after the ball slipped into his OWN net to put Tottenham ahead, before Matteus Tel doubled their lead.

Before it all fell apart, Palmer did his best to get down the right flank as he desperately searched for a lead against Spurs, who jump to 12th.

But Fernandes hit the deck amid the action and handled the ball.

The England international reacted with rage – creating a melee of stars from both sides.

Micky van de Ven saw yellow for arguing with referee Chris Kavanagh, while Fernandes was also handed the same punishment.

And, of course, Palmer.

Moments later, Penders’ slippy handling put the Blues on the back foot, throwing themselves into disarray.

And it got even worse after Palmer was shown a second yellow in the 80th minute for going in late on Conor Gallagher just outside the Chelsea box.

SkySports pundit Gary Neville could not believe his eyes when the No10 evaded punishment the first time round.

He said: “Palmer’s reaction was silly.

“He’s asking for trouble, that’s not great. No idea why he’s doing that.”

And fans rushed online to agree with the former Manchester United skipper, with one writing: “Should be a red for Palmer that he’s extremely lucky.”

Another said: “Palmer should be off. Absolute joke.”

A third asked: “How’s that not a RED CARD for Palmer???”

A fourth even noted: “Same mentality issues. He was lucky not to get two reds. Head loss.”

And after the sending off, Neville added: “Not a good challenge, naughty, really naughty.

“He’s spot on the referee, Cole Palmer has lost his head in the last ten minutes.

“That one is no brainer. That’s just a silly challenge, he knows exactly what he’s doing.”

Roberto De Zerbi’s Spurs look to be getting back on track, having beaten Coventry 3-1 on Monday, as they find themselves in their best league position since New Year’s Day.

The Lilywhites do not have a League Cup game mid-week after succumbing to a gutwrenching third round defeat to Liverpool last month.

Meanwhile, Xabi Alonso & Co’s slip up means they miss the opportunity to go level on points with third-place Brighton.

They will now travel to Anfield in the Carabao Cup’s fourth round, before hosting Man Utd next weekend.
